Insurance Claims Analysis (Power BI)

A four-page Power BI report on 2,000 insurance claims across six policy types and six branches, covering claims processing, profitability and fraud controls.

Report pages
Overview: claim volumes, amounts and status mix
Claims Processing: approval and rejection rates, settlement times, pending claims
Profitability: loss ratio and margin by policy type, branch and channel
Fraud and Controls: fraud rate, investigation funnel and fraud by claim reason
Key findings
Total claimed: AED 65.6M. 66% of claims were approved, 20% rejected and 14% pending (AED 9.1M).
Average settlement time was 23 days.
7% of claims were flagged as fraud (139 claims); Home Insurance was lowest at 3.8%.
Overall loss ratio was 72%. Business Insurance exceeded 100% (144%), while Travel was lowest (26%).
Only 32% of policies were renewed, with another 35% pending renewal.
Tools and methods

Power BI: data modelling, DAX measures (loss ratio, approval rate, fraud rate, average settlement days), slicers and interactive visuals.
