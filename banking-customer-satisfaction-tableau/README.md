**Banking Customer Satisfaction Analysis (Tableau)**

A Tableau analysis of 1,200 customer complaints from 2024, looking at how resolution speed, complaint type and channel affect customer ratings.

**Business questions**

Which complaint types take longest to resolve and leave customers least satisfied?
How does resolution time relate to customer rating?
Which channels perform best?

**Key findings**

65% of complaints were resolved (783 of 1,200); 241 were in progress and 176 pending.
Average resolution time was 27.7 hours (median 21 hours); 17% took longer than 48 hours.
Average customer rating was 3.2 out of 5; 45% of rated complaints were satisfied (4-5) and 34% dissatisfied (1-2).
Fraud Investigation complaints were the slowest (about 61 hours) and lowest rated (2.1).
Transaction Issue complaints were the fastest (about 7 hours) and highest rated (4.5).
Speed matters: complaints resolved within 48 hours averaged 3.5, against 1.8 for those over 48 hours.
Tools and methods

Tableau: calculated fields (resolution rate, over-48-hour %, satisfied and dissatisfied %), KPI tiles, dot plots, histograms, treemaps and monthly trends.
