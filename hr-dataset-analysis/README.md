HR Dataset Analysis (Excel)

An Excel analysis of workforce attrition, attendance and performance for a 2,500-employee dataset, built with PivotTables and an interactive dashboard.

Business questions
Where is attrition highest, by department and by location?
How do attendance, performance and training hours vary across the workforce?
When did employees leave, and did they resign or were they terminated?
Key findings
Attrition rate: 24.0% (601 of 2,500 employees have left; 1,899 are active).
Sales has the highest attrition (27.8%) and IT the lowest (21.9%).
By location, Sharjah is highest (25.4%) and Ajman lowest (22.0%).
480 exits were resignations and 121 were terminations. Exits peaked in 2024 (185).
Averages: performance score 76.5, attendance 87.5%, training 22.6 hours, manager rating 3.5 out of 5.
Tools and methods

Microsoft Excel: PivotTables, calculated KPIs, charts and a dashboard sheet.
